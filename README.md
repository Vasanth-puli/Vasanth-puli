## 🔧 Current focus: Anfrage-Check

An AI pre-check for technical customer inquiries (RFQs) at CNC machining companies.

Small manufacturers spend expensive engineering time reading emails, drawings and specifications before they can start costing. Anfrage-Check takes an RFQ as PDF or email text and returns, in about 15 seconds:

- every technical requirement, with the exact source wording
- a **complete / unclear / missing** status for each item
- the points to clarify before costing, ranked by impact on price and lead time
- a ready-to-send question to the customer, in German or English

The AI prepares; the engineer decides. I'm building it in public on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/vasanth-vk13-kumar/). Next up: scanned drawings, Excel attachments and pilot tests with manufacturers.
